🎯 Pronunciation & Sound Contrasts

1. Sound Contrast: FOOD /fuːd/ vs. FOOT /fʊt/

FOOD /fuːd/
Долгий глубокий звук /uː/ с вытянутыми в трубочку губами.

"I love good food and cozy cafes."

Слова с тем же звуком: spoon, smooth, cool, mood
FOOT /fʊt/
Короткий расслабленный звук /ʊ/ без вытягивания губ.

"I usually explore the city on foot."

Слова с тем же звуком: good, look, book, cook

Weekend Plans & Street Food: Emma, Ryan & Lucas

Emma (@emma.ny) and Ryan meet their friend Lucas at Chelsea Market to grab lunch and plan their Sunday afternoon. Listen carefully: they negotiate what to eat and what time works for everyone!

📍 Chelsea Market, Manhattan • Saturday 12:15 PM Natural Speech Pace

Ryan: "How about Mexican street tacos? They have amazing spicy beef tacos over there."
Lucas: "Oh, wait! I don't eat gluten or beef. What vegetarian food do they have?"

Ryan: "Perfect! Let's meet at Pier 57 at 2:15 PM!"

Step 3 • Grammar System & Guided Discovery

The Universal Formula for Open Questions: Q - A - S - V

To ask any open question in English, simply follow the 4-step sequence: Question Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb.

1. Question Word
Q
Where / What / When / How often
2. Auxiliary Verb
A
do / can / are
3. Subject
S
you / they / we
4. Main Verb
V
live / work / eat / order
WHERE Places & Locations
Где? Куда?

Ask about home, work, meeting spots, favorite spots:

Where do you live?
"I live alone in Brooklyn."
Where do you want to grab lunch?
"Let's go to Chelsea Market."
WHAT / WHAT TIME Objects & Hours
Что? Во сколько?

Ask about food, routine, schedules:

What do you usually order?
"I usually order avocado toast."
What time works for you?
"2:15 PM works great for me."
HOW OFTEN Frequency & Habits
Как часто?

Use frequency words: every day, on weekends, twice a week, rarely:

How often do you cook at home?
"I cook twice a week, and eat out on weekends."
How often do you travel abroad?
"Once or twice a year."
WOULD YOU LIKE / LET'S Invitations & Plans
Приглашения

Polite conversational phrases for inviting friends:

Would you like to grab lunch tomorrow?
"I'd love to!" / "I'd like to, but I'm busy."
Let's meet at 2:00 PM near the pier!
"Sounds like a plan!"
🔍

Teacher Concept Checks (CCQs) & Common Traps:

Trap 1: Missing "DO"

❌ "Where you live?" (Wrong!)
✔️ "Where do you live?" (Always include auxiliary!)

Trap 2: "What do you do?"

Does it mean what you are doing right now? No! It means your profession/occupation ("I'm a designer").

Trap 3: Falling Intonation ↘

In Yes/No questions voice goes UP (↗). In Wh- questions, voice goes DOWN at the end (↘)!
